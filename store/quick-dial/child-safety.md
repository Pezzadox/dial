# Quick Dial child safety standards

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Quick Dial is a dating app for adults aged 18 and over in the UK. We have zero tolerance for child
sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, and any
sexual content involving or aimed at anyone under 18.

## Adults only

Everyone must pass an age check (a selfie age estimate, with photo ID if it's close, run by Didit)
before anyone else can see their profile, and the app
refuses anyone who gives a date of birth under 18.

## What isn't allowed

Any content or behaviour that sexualises, exploits or endangers a child, including sharing, asking for
or linking to CSAM, and any attempt to contact or groom a child. Accounts that break this are removed
straight away and permanently.

## How to report

**In the app:** tap ··· on any profile or chat, then **Report**, and choose **May be under 18** or
another reason. Reports go to our safety team, and under-18 reports go to the top of the queue.
Reporters stay anonymous.

**Outside the app:** email [CONTACT EMAIL].

## What we do

Every photo and story is checked before anyone else sees it. When we find, or are told about, CSAE or
CSAM, we remove the content and the account, keep what the law requires us to keep, and report it to
the National Crime Agency (CEOP) and the Internet Watch Foundation, and to any other authority the law
requires. We cooperate with law enforcement.

We follow UK child safety law, including the Online Safety Act 2023.
