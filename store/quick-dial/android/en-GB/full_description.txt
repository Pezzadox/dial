Dating with fewer, better choices.

Quick Dial shows you ten verified people at a time. Tap a circle to see their photos, a short talking video and five stories. Pick one, then turn the Dial from 1 to 100 on each. If you both dial each other 70 or more, it's a mutual.

LOOKS, VIBE AND PERSONALITY
Your stories count as much as your photos. Short and specific wins.

EVERYONE IS VERIFIED
A quick selfie check confirms each person is real, matches their photos and is over 18. Every photo and story is checked before anyone sees it.

YOUR DIAL, PRIVATELY
See your own average once five people have dialled you, and how it changes over time. Nobody ever sees one person's score.

SAFE BY DESIGN
Report and block in two taps. Reporters stay anonymous. Your location is rounded to about a kilometre and people only ever see a distance.

No adverts. No selling your data. Delete your account any time from inside the app.

For adults aged 18 and over in the UK.
