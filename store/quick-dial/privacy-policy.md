# Quick Dial privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Quick Dial is a dating app for adults in the UK. This policy explains what we collect, why, who
else handles it, how long we keep it, and your rights.

**Who we are.** Quick Dial is run by Kyle Perry, trading as Pezzadox, the data controller.
Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL].

## What we collect and why

| What | Why | Legal basis (UK GDPR) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Email address | Signing in with a one-time code | Contract |
| First name, date of birth, gender, who you want to see, distance | Your profile and who we show you | Contract |
| Photos, a talking video, three photobooth selfies, five written stories | Your profile, which other members see and Dial | Contract |
| Rough location, rounded to about 1 km | Showing people nearby. Others only ever see a distance like "3 mi" | Contract |
| Dials you give and receive (scores from 1 to 100) | Matching, and your own Dial. Nobody ever sees one person's score; averages appear only once at least 5 people have dialled | Contract |
| Messages | Chatting with your connections | Contract |
| Age check result (over 18 or not) | Making sure everyone is over 18 | Legitimate interests and legal obligation |
| Reports and blocks | Keeping members safe | Legitimate interests |
| Push notification token | Telling you about mutuals and messages | Consent (you can switch notifications off on your phone) |

Who you want to see can reveal your sexual orientation, which is special category data. We use it
only to show you the right people, with your explicit consent given when you set it.

## Who else handles your data

- **Supabase** stores the database and your files, in the UK/EU.
- **Yoti** runs the age check. You choose how: a selfie, a photo of your ID, or the Yoti app. Yoti handles these under its own privacy notice and deletes selfies used to estimate age straight after the check. We receive only the result, never your selfie or ID.
- **Anthropic** checks new photos and stories against our rules before anyone sees them, and, only if you tap for reply ideas, suggests replies. The other person's messages are only included if they have switched that on. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.
- **Expo** delivers push notifications.
- **Apple and Google** handle any payments. We never see your card details.

We never sell your data and there are no adverts or advertising trackers in Quick Dial.

## How long we keep it

Everything stays while you have an account. When you delete your account (You → Delete account,
or by email, see below) we delete your profile, photos, videos, selfies, stories, Dials, chats,
connections, location and push token straight away. Backups roll over within 30 days.

One exception: if other members reported you, a record of those reports (the reason and what the
reporter wrote, not your photos or messages) is kept for 12 months so that deleting an account
cannot be used to hide harassment.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we
use it, or move it elsewhere. Email [CONTACT EMAIL]. If you are unhappy with our answer you can
complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

## Age

Quick Dial is for people aged 18 and over. Everyone passes an age check before being shown to others.

## Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and tell you in the app if the change matters.
