# Delete your Quick Dial account

**In the app:** open Quick Dial, tap **You**, scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete account**.
It happens straight away.

**Without the app:** email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address you sign in with, with the subject
"Delete my account". We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted: your profile, photos, video, selfies, stories, Dials (given and received),
chats, connections, location and notification settings.

What is kept: if other members reported you, a record of those reports (not your photos or
messages) for 12 months, for safety. Backups roll over within 30 days.
